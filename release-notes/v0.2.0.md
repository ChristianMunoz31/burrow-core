# Burrow 0.2.0

Burrow is a private budget and journal app for your own computer. Everything stays on your computer. There are no accounts and no tracking, and nothing goes online unless you turn it on yourself.

This is the first public release. It is a desktop app for Windows with a built-in helper named Javi.

## What you can do with Burrow

- Keep your budget and transactions in one place, and bring in transactions from a CSV file.
- Write a journal and keep notes, to-dos, habits, countdowns, bookmarks and a calendar (you can import `.ics` files).
- Track focus time. Connect weather and an Oura Ring if you want to. Both are off until you turn them on.
- Ask Javi for advice about the page you are on. Javi runs on your own computer through Ollama, a free program. Burrow walks you through setting it up the first time and recommends a model that fits your computer. You can skip it, and everything else works without it.
- Get an automatic backup of your data every day, saved on your computer. You can restore any backup with one click, and you can turn automatic backups off in Settings.
- Export all of your data to open formats (CSV, JSON and Markdown) and import it back at any time.

## Free and Pro

Burrow is free to download and use. Your data is never locked behind a payment: export, import, backups and privacy controls are always free.

Burrow Pro is an optional upgrade, a one-time purchase of $49 with no subscription. It includes a year of updates, and you keep the last version you received. Pro adds Javi's actions (Javi can look things up and make changes you approve), the daily briefing, and Explain on cross insights, where Javi describes a pattern between parts of your life (such as sleep and spending) in plain words.

- You can try Pro free for 7 days from inside the app. There is no account and no card.
- To upgrade, buy Burrow Pro at https://cdmunoz98.gumroad.com/, then paste the license key from your receipt into Settings, then License, and click Activate.
- Burrow contacts the store only when you click Activate. It never checks your license again.

## Download

- Windows PCs with an Intel or AMD processor (most PCs): [Burrow-Setup-x64.exe](https://github.com/ChristianMunoz31/burrow-releases/releases/download/v0.2.0/Burrow-Setup-x64.exe)
- Windows PCs with an ARM processor (for example Snapdragon laptops): [Burrow-Setup-arm64.exe](https://github.com/ChristianMunoz31/burrow-releases/releases/download/v0.2.0/Burrow-Setup-arm64.exe)

Not sure which one you have? Open Settings, then System, then About, and look at "System type".

Burrow installs for your user only and does not need administrator rights. If your PC does not have Microsoft Edge WebView2, the installer downloads it. This needs an internet connection once. Mac and Linux versions are planned for later.

## Windows may warn you the first time

These builds are not code-signed yet. Code signing is a planned future step. Until then Windows SmartScreen shows "Windows protected your PC" when you open the installer. This is expected. To continue:

1. Click **More info**.
2. Click **Run anyway**.

Only install Burrow files that you downloaded from this page.

## Check your download (optional)

Open PowerShell in the folder with the file and run:

```powershell
Get-FileHash .\Burrow-Setup-x64.exe -Algorithm SHA256
```

Use `Burrow-Setup-arm64.exe` instead if you downloaded the ARM installer. The hash it prints must match the line for your file in [SHA256SUMS.txt](https://github.com/ChristianMunoz31/burrow-releases/releases/download/v0.2.0/SHA256SUMS.txt). If it does not match, delete the file and download it again.

Your data stays on your computer. Updating or uninstalling Burrow keeps it. See all versions on the [releases page](https://github.com/ChristianMunoz31/burrow-releases/releases).
