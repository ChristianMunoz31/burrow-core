- Folders: pick a folder on your computer and Burrow lists its files, works out counts, sizes and duplicates, can write short summaries with Javi on your computer, and exports the list as Markdown, JSON or CSV. It only reads, and only the folders you choose.
- With Burrow Pro you can ask questions about a folder and get a report.
- PDF and Word files show as Not supported yet in Folders. Support for them is coming in a later version.
- Settings > Updates and Help > Check for updates show what's new and give you the installer with its SHA-256 checksum, so you can check the download.
- Javi answers faster on small models and sticks to your own numbers.
