You keep the copyright in Your Contributions. This agreement gives the Owner the rights needed to include them in Burrow, including in the proprietary official builds and in separately sold products.

## 1. Definitions

- **"Contribution"** means any original work of authorship, including code, tests, documentation and any modification of existing work, that You intentionally submit to the Owner for inclusion in Burrow core, for example as a pull request, patch, issue attachment or comment on the public repository. Work that You mark clearly in writing as "Not a Contribution" is excluded.
- **"Burrow"** means the Burrow core software published by the Owner and every product, build or kit that includes or derives from it.
- **"Submit"** means any form of electronic or written communication sent to the Owner or its repositories for the purpose of discussing or improving Burrow.

You represent that:

1. Each Contribution is Your original work, or You have the right to submit it under this agreement.
2. You are legally entitled to grant the licenses in sections 2 and 3. ## 7. Credit

When the Owner merges a Contribution on the public repository, the public history shows You as its author. When the Owner applies the same change to the private working repository, the commit carries a `Co-authored-by` trailer naming You.

## 8. Signing

You sign once. The signature covers all of Your past and future Contributions to Burrow core until You tell the Owner in writing that You withdraw for future Contributions. Withdrawal does not affect Contributions already submitted.

When You open a pull request, the CLA check (CLA Assistant Lite, a GitHub Action) comments on it if You have not signed. To sign, reply on the pull request with exactly this sentence:

```
I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA
```

The check records Your GitHub user name and the time of Your comment in a file on the `cla-signatures` branch of the public repository. If You contribute on behalf of a company, ask the Owner for a corporate agreement before You submit.
